# Wayfinder Project Lessons

Wayfinder is Pilot 1: a completed internal dogfood pilot, not a public release. Its prior Human Expansion Re-Playtest Gate remains `pending_human_expansion_replaytest`; closure does not turn unanswered questions into approval.

The machine-readable record is [wayfinder.project-lessons.json](wayfinder.project-lessons.json), and the closure decision is [wayfinder.pilot-closure.json](wayfinder.pilot-closure.json).

## Lessons

- `WL-01`: technically distinct branches did not initially produce felt agency or consequence.
- `WL-02`: active observation and persistent-world response improved the revised slice in human replay.
- `WL-03`: repeated three-way mechanics read as sameness despite content differences.
- `WL-04`: Choice Memory was perceptible and worth preserving.
- `WL-05`: correct state history alone did not make a journey; scene progression and narrative motion mattered separately.
- `WL-06`: material agent-authored improvements required another Human Gate; machine QA never became experience approval.

## Candidate Rules and promotion

`CR-01` through `CR-03` are candidate rules only. Wayfinder alone cannot promote them into global Studio skills. Promotion requires evidence from a materially different later pilot, explicit human validation with strong cross-context rationale, or controlled comparative evidence.

## Reuse boundary and Pilot 2

Evidence/provenance, rights, originality, and Human Gate boundaries are reusable now. Wayfinder's content, SVG world, and state machine remain pilot-specific. Interaction lessons remain candidates until replicated.

Pilot 2 must be materially different in primary user goal, interaction primitive, information architecture, and visual problem, preferably with different rendering needs, while retaining the Reference → Evidence → Synthesis → Human Gate methodology. No Pilot 2 has been selected or implemented.
